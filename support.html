Does Moss cost anything?

No. It's free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Can Moss stop me using other apps?

No. It can't see or restrict your other apps, so sticking to the plan is up to you.

I'm not getting reminders

Open Settings > Notifications > Moss and check that notifications are allowed. Also check whether a Focus mode is silencing them.

The widget isn't updating

iOS decides how often widgets refresh, so it may lag a little. Opening the app usually updates it. If it stays stuck, remove the widget and add it again.

I got a new phone. Can I keep my progress?

Your progress is stored on your device, so it moves over only if you restore from a backup of your old iPhone. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted with it.

Does leaving the app during a focus session count against me?

No. You may get a nudge and some teasing, but no penalty, and the app can't see where you went. Choosing "Give up" costs a small mood drop.

What does the rough day pass do?

Moss goes easy on you today and your streak stays safe. Any focus you finish still counts.

Does Moss see my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
