Moss Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy covers the Moss app ("the app") and its developer ("we"). The short version: your data stays on your iPhone. We have no accounts and no servers, and we don't collect your personal data.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following on your device only, and doesn't send any of it to us:

  • Your check-ins: what you tell the app about your phone day, including your answers during setup
  • Your focus sessions: how long they ran, whether you finished, and whether you left the app during one
  • Your progress: your sprout's mood, growth, streaks, outfits and personal bests
  • Your settings: your focus goal, tone and evening check-in choice

The app can't see your other apps or how you use your phone. If you leave during a focus session, it knows only that you left, not where you went.

Notifications (optional)

If you allow them, the app sends an evening check-in, a message when a focus session ends, and a nudge if you leave during a session. They're created on your device. You can turn them off in Settings at any time.

Information Apple may provide

If you agree to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymized crash reports and usage statistics. We use them only to fix problems and improve the app. We can't identify you from them. See Apple's privacy policy.

Device backups

Data the app stores on your device may be included in your iCloud or computer backups, depending on your settings. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, and we can't access them.

Tracking and advertising

The app has no ads and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and rights

Since we don't hold your personal data, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. To remove your data, delete the app from your device.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data. You can contact us with any privacy question at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
